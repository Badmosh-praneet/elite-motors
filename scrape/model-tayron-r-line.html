Tayron R-Line

Designed for those who seek performance with sophistication, the Volkswagen Tayron R-Line transforms every drive into a dynamic experience. With its bold R-Line styling, athletic stance, and premium craftsmanship, the Tayron R-Line delivers a perfect balance of power, comfort, and advanced technology. Engineered for confidence and control, it offers a refined driving experience whether navigating city streets or embarking on long journeys, making every moment behind the wheel truly exhilarating.

Safety, the Volkswagen Way

Built with Volkswagen’s uncompromising commitment to safety, the Tayron R-Line is engineered to deliver confidence on every drive. With a robust body structure, advanced safety systems, and intelligent driver-assistance technologies, the Tayron R-Line is designed to protect what matters most. For us at Volkswagen, safety remains our guiding principle—ensuring peace of mind for you and your passengers, wherever the road leads.


Room for big plans and everything that comes with them.

The Volkswagen Tayron R-Line is designed for people who move between roles and routines and moments without stopping to switch gears. It brings together confident design and composed performance and generous space and intuitive technology in a way that feels effortless.
