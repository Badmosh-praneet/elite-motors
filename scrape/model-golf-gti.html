Golf GTI

Pre-Book Now

The VW Golf GTI is here. You’re welcome.

You already know. The look. The power. The legacy.
No need for small talk, just drive.

Enough said.


Looking for details?

We could’ve left it at the badge.
But if you’re after the finer print, here’s what went into earning it.




Gallery

Performance
Fast. Loud. Fun. What else do you need?
  • 2.0L TSI engine: It moves.
  • 0-100 km/h in 5.9 seconds: It moves fast.
  • 370 Nm Torque: It pulls.
  • 265 Horsepower: It really pulls.

Technology
Are you not done geeking out yet?
  • 32.8 cm (12.9-inch) touchscreen infotainment: Tap. Swipe. Drive.
  • Digital Cockpit Pro with 26.04 cm (10.25-inch) customizable display: All the info. All at once.
  • Seven-speaker immersive sound: Every drive needs a soundtrack.
  • Wireless charging: Ditch the cables. Keep the energy.
  • IDA Voice Control & Voice Enhancer: Talk to your car. It actually listens.
  • Wired & Wireless App-Connect: Stay connected - or off the grid.

Exterior
You won’t see much of it. You’ll be in it. But for everyone else.
  • Illuminated Volkswagen logo, Signature red GTI accents: So you know it’s special.
  • Exterior ambient lighting with wake-up & goodbye effect: Goodbyes should look cool too.
  • X-shaped fog lights: Because round ones are boring.
  • 18-inch “Richmond”, diamond-turned surface wheels: Black, bold and hard to ignore.
  • Twin chrome exhausts: Power you can hear.

Safety

This hatch has your back. And front. And sides.
  • Seven airbags: Thrill with a safety net.
  • XDS electronic front differential lock: Grip matters.
  • Adaptive Cruise Control : Fast, but not reckless.
  • Lane Assist: Knows where you’re going. And who’s next to you.
  • Front Assist - Emergency braking with pedestrian and cyclist monitoring: Not everyone moves as fast as you.
  • Rear-view camera & Park Distance Control: For when you're moving slow for a change.

Interior
Buttons, screens, lights - the fancy stuff.
But really, just get your hands on the wheel already.

  • Scalepaper Plaid seats with red accents: Old-school cool.
  • 30-colour ambient lighting: For 30 shades of mood.
  • Leather-wrapped sports steering wheel with GTI clasp: So, you grip it right before you rip it.
  • Paddle shifts: Flick, shift, gone.
  • Panoramic sunroof: Sometimes, you’ll want to slow down.

Comfort and Convenience
Does a hot hatch need to be comfortable? Maybe.
Did we do it anyway? Of course.

  • Three-zone Climatronic AC: Your temp. Their temp. No debates.
  • Progressive steering: Because you shouldn’t have to wrestle it.
  • Rear AC vents with temperature control: Even your backseat friends deserve some air.
  • Keyless Start with pulse effect and SAFELOCK: Press. Fire up. Go.
