# Insurance

Your motor insurance includes exclusive benefits along with better and more extensive coverage. It ensures constant support with a 24*7 dedicated call center and fast and simple processing for customers without additional work and expenses.

## Benefits

- Exclusive benefits package
- Extensive coverage
- 24/7 dedicated call centre support
- Fast and simple claim processing
- Minimal customer effort required
- Cashless settlement at authorised workshops
- Volkswagen Genuine Parts used on every claim

## Fields collected on the renewal form

- Select Vehicle Model
- Select Registration Year
- Select Registration Month
- Registration Number
- Full Name
- Mobile
- Email
- Location
- Insurance Company
- Policy Number
- Insurance Expiry Date
