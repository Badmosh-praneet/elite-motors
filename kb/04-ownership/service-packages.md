# Service Value Packages

With our promise to deliver complete peace of mind for each of our customers, we have created Service Value Packages, that promise just that. These packages ensure your car is looked after by experts year-after-year without you having to worry about increasing costs. So while we add life to your car, you add money to your wallet with Volkswagen Service Value Packages.

- Protection against labour and spare part cost inflation
- 100% genuine parts guaranteed
- Diagnosis and servicing by Certified Technicians only
- Stress-free drives with complete peace of mind
